Source: source-data CSVs included in 04_Figure_Exact_Data. These prompts are design specifications, not authority to invent measurements.

## Shared art direction
Academic journal-grade infographics on a clean white background with high information density but generous negative space. Colors: navy #17324D, teal #1A918D, sky blue #518CC6, ochre #D39A38, violet #7161AA; residual categories gray #839099. Use thin purposeful connectors, strong typographic hierarchy, English labels, accessible contrast and compact legends. Prefer landscape 16:9 for process diagrams and landscape A4 for taxonomic trees. Export lossless PNG at 300 dpi and editable SVG. All numeric content must come solely from the associated source CSV. Do not add icons, environmental claims, scenario percentages or made-up sample data.
